Flags in icons/HD/e2ivk/flags, icons/FHD/e2ivk/flags and icons/WQHD/e2ivk/flags
==================================================================================

The following flags were rendered from the SVGs of flag-icons
(https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons, v7.5.0, flags/4x3) and styled like the
other flags of the on-screen keyboard (gloss and edge):

dv_MV fo_FO ha_NG hy_AM kk_KZ kl_GL km_KH ky_KG lo_LA mn_MN ne_NP ps_AF
si_LK syr_SY tg_TJ tk_TM ur_PK uz_UZ wo_SN zh_HK zh_MO zh_SG
dz_BT tzm_DZ ff_GN ku_IQ tzm_MA my_MM gn_PY so_SO

sr_CS (Serbia and Montenegro) is a self-drawn tricolour (public-domain
design) using flag-icons' colours.
